# Why this shape

The reference spread on one tiny, universally known disaster repeated across eras with a sci-fi twist at the end, so we kept that structure with a disaster everyone has had, made each era's loss the punchline, ended on a zero-g twist, and added what a generated clip can't do: you can knock the cup over yourself.
